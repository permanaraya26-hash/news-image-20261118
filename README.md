Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty [Sum up 18 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118) — $250 USDC, top 50 paid.

A program that writes one 1024×1024 image summing up the world's news of Wednesday 18 November 2026 to `/output/entry.png`: a newspaper front page — the U.S. sinking five Iranian tankers in the Gulf of Oman, Bahrain intercepting a third Iranian drone attack in five days, the U.N. warning of deaths from the cold in Gaza, the Kerch bridge road-arch collapse (as a 16–17 November follow-up), the Quad meeting in Manila, and Cyclone Akvan bearing down on India. Also today: the COP31 draft deal of 144 items under fire in Antalya, and Robert Pattinson named the new James Bond.

Rebuilt 19 November 2026 with the actual 18 November news (the first entry, filed early on 18 November, was mostly 17 November news).

Run it with `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`. Deterministic: every run writes the same bytes, with no network.
